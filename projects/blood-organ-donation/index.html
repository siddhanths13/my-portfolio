Become a donor

Register as a blood donor

Takes about a minute. You're eligible if you're 18–65, weigh at least 45 kg and are generally healthy.

Quick eligibility check

  • Age between 18 and 65 years
  • Body weight at least 45 kg
  • Haemoglobin level ≥ 12.5 g/dl
  • No cold / flu in the last 2 weeks
  • Not pregnant or breastfeeding
  • No new tattoo / piercing in last 6 months

The body replaces donated blood within 24–48 hours. Men can donate every 3 months, women every 4.

Organ & tissue donation

One organ donor can save up to eight lives. Pledging your organs is simple, legal and free.

What can be donated?

Kidneys 2 lives Liver can be split Heart Lungs 2 lives Pancreas Intestine Corneas sight for 2 Skin & bone

In India, anyone can pledge to be an organ donor. Your family's consent is the final word, so talk to them about your decision — that conversation matters as much as the pledge.
